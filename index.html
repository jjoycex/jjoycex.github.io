I am Co-President of the Science Olympiad team at Great Neck South High School. At the 2026 Science Olympiad National Tournament, I placed 9th in Experimental Design, 15th in Robot Tour, 16th in Electric Vehicle, and 29th in Helicopter.

My Hofstra University summer research paper on low-energy gridshell fabrication has been published to the 2026 IASS-IWSS Annual Symposium, and my engineering research on an assistive animatronic sign-language hand won 3rd Place in the Final Round of LISEF (Long Island Science & Engineering Fair).

Most recently, I completed the Basics of ASICs (Application-Specific Integrated Circuits) program with multiple awards at the MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute (BWSI).

In my spare time, I love playing the piano and violin, reading, and sewing. All of my engineering and research projects are open-sourced on GitHub.
